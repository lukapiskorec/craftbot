Images only: 28 views, legend `views_fable.py`, `requirements.md` v2, `concept.md` v2, reference images in `input/`. First rendered version, no previous inspection. Camera convention used below: azimuth 270 is the camera at low y (ESE side); 225 and 315 also see the y_min face, 45 and 135 the y_max face. In view 05 (top) +y is up, so the strips are C (top), B (middle), A (bottom).

## Summary

- Open defects: 9 new findings (B stair block above the road-level roof, view 07/25; C stair 2 guard hides its flight and shows outside the closed wall, views 22/26; full-height glass panel in A's stair hall, view 06; terrace end walls at x = 65/66 not legible in any view, views 10/12/16-18; terrace guards visible with Glazing hidden, views 16-18; dark patch in B's skin around the pavilion and along the retaining walls to about x = 20, views 05/12; dark solid square under A's pavilion at the box-level cut, view 11; C plan views 13-15 cut at L0/L1/L2 + 1.5 by the evidence, no L3 plan; small skin wedge at C's x = 29 corner, view 23), plus 6 Builder-known items of which 1 is confirmed visible (A terrace glass and closed panel).
- Fixed since last version: none (first version).
- Requirements rejected: R-20 (guards), R-40 (B stair block stands above ground at the road), R-48 (no C L3 interior plan by the evidence of views 13-15); R-08, R-23, R-27, R-29, R-39, R-46 confirmed in part only (end walls at the terrace and A's five risers not verifiable at the rendered scale).
