## Proposed changes (Designer to rule; each with the view and the sketch that motivate it)

1. Pavilion offset (row 8; sketch 01 and site_01 against view 05). The sketches show the pavilion's ESE face flush with the bar's ESE face and the pavilion wider than the bar, projecting 2.6 m on the WNW side. The model shifts a 6 x 6 square 2 m WNW, which leaves a 2 m step on the ESE side that no sketch has. Closest reading with the 6 m bar: pavilion on y = 7..15 (6 along x, 8 across), ESE face flush, 2 m WNW projection.
2. Open side of segment 2 starting uphill (row 18; sketch 02 against views 02, 06, 07, 08). The WNW green starts at 21 m, not 28; the stretch 21..28 (bays 23..27 in the model's post rhythm) is open on both sides in the sketch.
3. Open side of segment 3 running to the house end (row 19; sketch 02 against views 24, 25, 26). The ESE green runs to 65.8 m; in the model the ESE side closes at 60 with a strip window. The last bay 60..66 is open on both sides in the sketch. This puts core 4 (the ensuite) against a glass wall in all three variations, which is the reason the Designer may keep the panel instead.
4. Pavilion ESE face (row 17; sketch 02 against views 24, 25, 26). The ESE green starts at 4.9 m and covers the pavilion. Either glaze the pavilion's ESE face or record that a garage face stays closed.
5. Switch character (row 21; sketch 02). Changes 2 and 3 together turn the model's clean corners at 28 and 60 into the sketch's both-sides-open zones; the corner at 44 stays a corner (the sketch's 2 m overlap there is within its own line noise).

## Kept-with-reason candidates (the reason as read from the images and the concept)

- Plot taper 16.8 to 20.1 m dropped (row 2): the house never reaches a long boundary; in the ledger.
- Ground steepening from 50 (survey) not 67 (sketches 07, 08) (row 4): sketch 09's ground agrees with the survey within 0.3 m at x = 66 anyway; in the ledger.
- Pavilion 6.0 m long against 4.9 to 5.4 in the sketches (row 6): the garage door and the 36 m2 programme; 0.6 to 1.1 m over tolerance, so it needs the reason written down.
- The 8 to 10 m gap between the pavilion and the bar in sketches 01, 03 to 05 and site_01 (row 10): sketch 06 is the adopted plan and closes the gap. Sketch 02's ESE green runs across the gap, so if the gap ever returns it is an open court, not a wall.
- Bar to 94 m in sketch 06 (row 12): a level bar to 94 stands 10 m clear at its end; sketch 01 and the sections end the house at 66 to 70, which the model matches.
- Bar 6.0 m wide against 3.7 (row 13): open plan; in the ledger.
- Box height 3.8 to 4.4 m against 5.0 (rows 15, 33): S-06 states 3.3 clear.
