# Sources, experiment 15 (Koreni), Fable run

Researcher pass 1, 2026-09-09, against `concept.md` version 1. Manuals consulted: *Architect's Handbook of Construction Detailing* (AHCD, 2nd ed. 2009; page = printed page, PDF page = printed + 17 in ch. 1 to 3, + 16 in ch. 5 to 6), *Canadian Wood-Frame House Construction* (CMHC, 2013; PDF page = printed + 19), *London Housing Design Guide* (LHDG, interim 2010; PDF page = printed page). All three PDFs were opened at the pages cited; the timber manuals other than CMHC were not opened (nothing in their index descriptions touches a concrete, steel and glass house).

Datum conventions: "above roof surface" means above the finished roof membrane, not the structural slab; "below grade" means below finished ground at the wall face; footing depth is to the footing underside; stair rise is floor finish to floor finish.
