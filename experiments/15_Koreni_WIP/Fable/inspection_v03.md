Images only: 40 views, legend `views_fable.py`, `requirements.md` v2, `concept.md` v2, reference images in `input/`, previous inspection `inspection_v01.md` (no v02 inspection was made, so this one covers what v02 would have). ## Summary

- Open defects: 2 geometry findings (1 new: a door-sized opening in the segment-4 closed wall near the house end in all three variations, views 24/25/26/35/36; 1 carried from v01: the dark slot at the top of C's segment-1 closed wall at x = 28, views 22/39), 1 view gap (view 29 does not show A's five risers; the slab edge hides them at 20 degrees elevation), 1 item reported for the Designer (B's high bay has full-height glass on its ESE face, no spandrel, views 25/40).
- Fixed since last version: all nine v01 findings (B stair block redefined as the high bay and moved to R-40; C stair 2 guard; A stair-hall panel; terrace end walls; terrace guards with Glazing hidden; B skin dark patch; dark square under A's pavilion; C plan levels; C x = 29 skin wedge) and all six Builder-known items (C storefront heads, A dogleg guards, floor beam across the void, B stair through the x = 15 wall, A terrace glass and panel, A end wall at x = 11).
- Requirements rejected: none. R-07, R-12, R-20, R-27, R-29, R-35, R-39, R-46 confirmed; R-08 and R-19 confirmed in part (B's and C's door from the pavilion into the bar is seen in no view; study, utility and store are not distinguishable by eye); R-40 and R-45 reported, not judged.
