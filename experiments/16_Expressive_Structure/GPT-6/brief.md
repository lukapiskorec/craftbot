# Brief as understood

This single-variation run builds a square room twice as tall as its clear width, with one entrance and daylight from the roof or openings close to it. Thin timber slats form the enclosure and the exterior structure. The red building photographs guide construction character and scale; the other two images guide a series of undulating trusses that give the outside an expressive profile while preserving a simple interior. The 2:1 proportion applies to the clear room, with the exterior trusses allowed to project beyond it. No reference feature is deliberately dropped at this stage; dimensions inferred from photographs must be identified as estimates.

## Concept-stage scope decisions

The adopted clear dimensions are 3.00 x 3.00 x 6.00 m. A provisional 3.60 x 3.60 x 7.20 m scheme was reduced after the photo calibration suggested about 2.8 m exterior width with substantial perspective uncertainty. The adopted width is a design estimate close to that source; the exact 2:1 height follows the user even where the reference appears less tall. Seven truss frames are spaced at 0.50 m.

Open high clerestories provide daylight without glazing. This is a conceptual timber pavilion enclosure; weatherproofing is not established. Ground support, fixings, member capacities, and hold-down design remain unverified. These performance limits do not waive geometric bearing, contact, bracing, or visual inspection requirements. A usable 0.90 x 2.10 m entrance deliberately replaces the unusually low reference aperture. The second and third structural images guide timber geometry; their glazing and unrelated enclosure materials are not copied.
