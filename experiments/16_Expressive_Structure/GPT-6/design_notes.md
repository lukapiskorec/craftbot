## 2026-09-14 — v01 concept: two envelopes

The five images share a useful distinction: a calm enclosure can sit within a much more active external assembly. Adopt a flat red square room and put changing depth in exterior column trusses and roof trusses. Rejected a wavy inner wall/ceiling because the brief expressly asks for the inside to remain simple. ## 2026-09-14 — v01 concept: thin slats and load paths

The repeated catalogue is24x90mm boarding and36x90mm structural slats. Paired chords bracket a single web layer; deep truss action comes from separated chords, not a solid thick beam. Roof reactions go to the paired side-column heads, down triangulated inner/outer chords to sole runners and a crossed slat mat. Longitudinal braces tie frame stations to ground; explicit floor/roof diagonals collect lateral action. Rejected conventional glulam posts, plywood gussets and steel brackets as modeled elements because they would undermine the entirely-thin-timber brief. Concealed fasteners and anchors are acknowledged as unresolved construction necessities.

The manuals establish only analogies: CMHC19mm boards at600mm support spacing and FRIM35x72mm truss members over5.77m. Our24mm boards at500mm spacing and36x90 paired chords are mine, not manual-certified. The6m braced columns, connections, base anchors and soil interface have no matching prescriptive design. No structural adequacy claim is made.

## 2026-09-14 — v01 concept: fixed faceted expression

Seven frames at500mm spacing carry an additive roof height field: a low curved profile sampled across x plus a changing vertical offset along y. Each interval remains a planar facet, so straight short slats can close the roof and bear at their ends. The outer side-column nodes also vary in depth while their inner chords stay vertical. Rejected an arbitrary double-curved skin that would force twisted long boards; rejected a simple pitched roof repeated unchanged because the two expressive references ask for stronger articulation.

All characteristic profiles and paired chord layer offsets are fixed in concept.md before geometry. A Builder may fit and clip joints within those profiles; changing the room, number of ribs, exterior expression or member catalogue is a design question.

## 2026-09-14 — v01 concept: enclosure and daylight scope

Opaque lower walls concentrate light at0.75m-high clerestory bands immediately under the6m ceiling on both side walls. End walls remain full-height red boards, as in the interior photo. The roof is fully covered by slats and ceiling is a level slat plane. No glass or membrane is modeled. CraftBot confirmed the open-air pavilion reading on2026-09-14: this is a geometric architectural proposal with no demonstrated rain-tightness or conditioned-building performance. Rejected adding glazing merely to make the model appear weatherproof.

## 2026-09-14 — v01 build clarification: sole-runner datum

Accepted the Builder's integrated upper-layer sole runner assembly. Continuous longitudinal S runners occupy z=-0.060..-0.024, and upper transverse sleeper segments stop at their faces. Column feet remain at z=-0.024. Lower longitudinal sleepers sit directly beneath the runners and every foot footprint, carrying the load from foot to runner to lower sleeper to grade without a thin-slat cantilever. Floor support spacing remains <=0.500 m; fitted butt contacts replace intersecting volumes, with concealed connection design still unverified. This clarifies R-16 and the SOLE_RUNNERS row without changing stock or clear room dimensions.

Rejected placing a third 36 mm sole layer above the mat because it would raise feet to z=+0.012 and contradict the released COLUMN_Z datum. Rejected omitting the runner because a continuous bearing and tying line is part of the stated base assembly. The accepted arrangement retains that line within the existing 96 mm ground-to-floor stack; the crossing mat is continuous through the lower layer where upper transverse pieces are interrupted.
