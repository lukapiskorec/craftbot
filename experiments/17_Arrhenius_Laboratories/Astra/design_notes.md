## 2026-09-16 — prebuild concept, v01

**Decision:** Reconstruct the entire ring building, its low courtyard insert, four curved stairs and minimal internal organization. A facade-only sample was rejected because the plan and article establish the complete spatial arrangement. Fine laboratory equipment is outside the documented reference scope.

**Evidence hierarchy and levels:** Image08 section and photo06 independently show five occupied window bands; HIC calls it four-storey. Coordinator resolved this in favour of primary drawings/photos: ground plus four upper occupied levels, plant additional. Four total levels was rejected because it erases a clearly visible band. An invented sixth occupied floor was rejected. Whether the article excludes the ground level is unknown, not asserted as fact.

**Grid and scale:** Counted19 longitudinal bays and6 transverse bays; researcher confirmed both. Selected estimated6m/9m modules, yielding54x114m axes and3.6m storeys to18m roof. Plan aspect differs by about2% and section height ratio by about4%. Exact millimetric survey accuracy is not claimed. Using a photographed distant person to scale the whole building was rejected because the depth planes differ. Published total floor area was not used to back-solve a false precision because the included basement/associated area is unspecified.

**Facade system:** Exterior shafts and chamfered shoulders remain structurally legible. Long sides use precast spandrels and timber-framed windows. South facade is treated independently: deep metal fascia, recessed ribbon, fine lower horizontal zone and a glass slope rising outward over a porch. A generic five-strip facade on the south was rejected as contrary to02/05.

**Internal structure:** Inferred concrete service cores adjacent to open stairs provide x/y load paths and reach the roof. Exposed seated corbel connections are not labelled moment frames. Foundations, reinforcement and diaphragm capacity are unverified; represented sections are plausible estimates only. The concrete detailing manual offers no applicable member sizing, so timber span rules were rejected.

**Courtyard:** Photo04 establishes a partial low insert; its exact footprint and grade datums are estimated. Place it in the northern half against the west wing, retaining south open garden and east passage. The two-storey insert and grade change are text-supported; dimensions and stair route are inferred. Covering the whole court was rejected.

**Colour and detail:** Monochrome images establish tonal/material contrasts, while the article confirms concrete and wood. Chosen muted colours are mine. Window fine divisions, room assignments, roof plant positions and curved stair dimensions are estimates; their principal topology follows references. Maintain a small family of repeated sizes.
