## Per-view findings

- **Views 01–04 (orbits):** The five occupied levels, projecting exterior shafts/corbels, continuous roof slabs, and sparse plant boxes read consistently. The long courtyard is visibly open to sky, with the partial insert only at the far end; no off-site building mass or floating roof member is apparent.
- **View 05 (top):** The main roof ring and courtyard void are continuous, while the insert occupies only the northern courtyard portion and leaves the long southern garden open. Four stair locations are legible as openings/landing sectors.
- **View 16 (side elevation):** Five occupied window bands and a plant zone above are visible; the roof remains a flat ring. Repeated columns, spandrels, and window units continue across the level-4 facade without an obvious gap.
- **View 24 (roof plan):** All four curved stair openings are present within the ring and remain separated from the courtyard void. The west/east stairs sit close to their outer facades; plan view alone cannot establish the required exit width.
- **View 10 (structural cutaway):** The exposed frame shows continuous columns, beams, floor edges, cores, and curved stairs with open wells; no stair is visibly detached from its surrounding floor system in this view.
- **View 14 (facade detail):** Exterior columns stand proud of the enclosure and carry enlarged/chamfered shoulder pieces. Horizontal slab/spandrel bands, regular panel joints, broad window units, and narrow vertical lights are readable; Workbench shading does not prove transparent glass behaviour.
- **View 29 (west top-floor plan):** The west stair has a complete circular opening and finite tread/landing geometry. Its upper landing terminates toward the very narrow facade-side strip, with the usable route visually pinched; the required 1.20 m clear exit and 0.15 m non-connecting separation are not verifiable from this plan.
