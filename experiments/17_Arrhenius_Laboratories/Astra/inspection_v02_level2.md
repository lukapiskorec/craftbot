# Inspection v02 — occupied level 2

## summary

- Shared views 01–05, 14 and 22 show the building fully on the site, with no visible offsite mass; the four-storey courtyard-covering defect from v01 is fixed.
- The courtyard is open to sky except for the northern partial insert; its rooflight grid and narrow east passage are visible in views 01, 04, 05 and 14.
- Views 14 and 17 show four curved stair stacks with open wells and dark rails; exact headroom, 1.20 m route widths, bearing and clearance cannot be confirmed from renders.
- Views 16 and 17 show five occupied facade bands, repeated columns/corbels, beams, slabs, panels and glazing; side cladding reads structurally complete.
- View 17 provides an exposed frame/stair-gallery cutaway; glazing transmission/opacity is uncertain in these Workbench renders, and internal room topology and continuous circulation cannot be judged from this subset.
- Exact 19-by-6 counts, 22 risers and 5% proportions remain unverified by eye; the final 31,149/0/0 diagnostic summary cannot prove absence or route widths.

## per-view findings

- **View 01:** Elevated overview shows both long wings and end links enclosing a long open court. The roof is closed around the court, while the court itself remains open; no member visibly projects beyond the site strip.
- **View 02:** Opposite elevated overview confirms the same closed building ring and open court from the other diagonal. Roof plant is sparse, although several box forms are visible rather than only one family.
- **View 03:** South-oblique overview shows the recessed south porch/glazed front, projecting front columns and a partial court insert. The lower porch and stair/courtyard areas are not visibly floating.
- **View 04:** South-west overview shows the special south facade, open court, rooflight strip and east-side insert route. Glass surfaces are visible, but Workbench shading does not establish transmission versus opaque treatment.
- **View 05:** Top view shows a continuous perimeter roof/slab ring with a large open court, northern insert rooflight grid and no obvious slab infill across the long court. Four stair voids are not visible in this roof-level view.
- **View 16:** Long side elevation has five occupied bands, continuous horizontal spandrels, repeated window modules, projecting columns and corbel shoulders. The insert/gallery glazing is legible near the right end; no open facade envelope gap is apparent.
- **View 22:** Stair/top plan shows four separate circular stair locations within the two wings and links, all inside the building footprint. The southern stair is in the south link, but exact corridor clearance and landing connection cannot be measured.
- **View 14:** Close side facade shows consistent panel joints, window units and projecting chamfered corbels at every band. It confirms concrete frame and glazing separation but cannot establish minimum beam-seat overlap.
- **View 17:** Transverse cutaway shows four stacked open curved stair flights with dark railings, finite landings, open wells and exposed floor beams. Stair geometry reads as connected, but headroom and non-connecting rail/enclosure clearance are not measurable; glazing transmission remains uncertain.
- **View 19:** Structural cutaway shows repeated columns, transverse beams and slab edge members with stair flights visible behind. No obviously isolated stair or offsite member is visible; actual foundations and concealed connections remain unjudgeable.
