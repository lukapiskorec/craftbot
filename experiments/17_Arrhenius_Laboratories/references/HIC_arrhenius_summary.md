HIC describes a prefabricated kit of five elements: exterior pillars, post-tensioned beams, floor slabs, spiral stairs and structural façade plates. The exterior skin is insulated precast sandwich panels with horizontal fixed and operable wood-framed windows. The plan has two north–south wings linked by two narrow east–west elements around a narrow outdoor courtyard. A skylit two-storey room is inserted in the courtyard, with outdoor space remaining around it. The article calls the building four-storey; supplied image 08 and photo 06 govern the five occupied bands used in the model. HIC provides topology and material relationships, no dimensions.
